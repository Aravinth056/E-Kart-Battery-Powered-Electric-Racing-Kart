# E-Kart Components

## 1. Chassis

- Material: AISI 4130
- Tube Outer Diameter: 25.4 mm
- Tube Thickness: 1.6 mm
- Yield Strength: 435 MPa
- Ultimate Tensile Strength: 560 MPa
- Young's Modulus: 205 GPa
- Density: 7850 kg/m³
- Poisson's Ratio: 0.29

## 2. Electric Motor

- Model: DATAI 60-5000-150
- Type: PMSM
- Power: 5 kW
- Maximum Allowed Power: 6 kW
- Voltage: 60 V DC
- Phase: 6-Phase
- Maximum Design RPM: 3800
- Peak Torque: 58 Nm
- Continuous Torque: 16 Nm

## 3. Motor Controller

- Type: PMSM Controller
- Manufacturer: DATAI
- Peak Discharge: 10 kW
- Continuous Discharge: 5–6 kW

## 4. Battery Pack

- Type: Lithium-ion
- Chemistry: NMC
- Nominal Voltage: 60 V
- Fully Charged Voltage: 67.2 V
- Capacity: 90 Ah
- Configuration: Approximately 16S × 30P
- Cell Voltage: 3.7 V nominal
- Cell Voltage Range: 3.0–4.2 V
- Peak Discharge: Approximately 9 kW
- Continuous Discharge: Approximately 5 kW
- Cooling: Passive air cooling
- Protection: BMS

## 5. DC-DC Converter

- Input Voltage: 48–72 V
- Output Voltage: 12 V
- Output Current: 15 A
- Purpose: Supplies the auxiliary low-voltage electrical system

## 6. Chain Drive

- Motor Sprocket: 12 Teeth
- Final Sprocket: 48 Teeth
- Final Drive Ratio: 4:1
- Drive Shaft Diameter: 25 mm
- Keyway: 8 × 7 mm
- Key Type: Parallel Key

## 7. Braking System

- Brake Position: Rear
- Disc Diameter: 220 mm
- Disc Thickness: 4.0 mm
- Brake Pedal Ratio: 4–5:1
- Human Pedal Force: Approximately 300–400 N

## 8. Wheels and Tyres

### Front

- Size: 10 × 4.7 × 5 in

### Rear

- Size: 11 × 7.1 × 5 in

## 9. Steering System

- Mechanical Linkage Ratio: 10:1
- Ackermann Percentage: 37.5%
- Turning Radius: 2.7 m
- Lock-to-Lock: -230° to +230°

## 10. Electrical Components

- 150 A Automotive Blade Fuse
- 150 A DC MCB
- Accumulator Isolation Relay (AIR)
- High-Voltage DC Contactor
- Anderson SB 175 A Connector
- Auxiliary 12 V Battery
- 60–72 V to 12 V DC-DC Converter
- Ignition Key Switch
- Tractive System Disconnect
- Digital LCD

## 11. Safety Components

- Driver Kill Switch

- External Kill Switch
- TSAL Red LED
- RTDS Buzzer
- Battery Management System (BMS)
- Fire Extinguisher
- Driver Helmet
