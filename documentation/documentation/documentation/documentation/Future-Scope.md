# Future Scope

The E-Kart can be further improved in the following areas:

- **Powertrain Optimization**
  - Improve motor and gear ratio selection.
  - Reduce energy losses in the power transmission system.
  - Improve overall vehicle efficiency and range.

- **Battery System Improvement**
  - Improve battery monitoring and protection.
  - Enhance Battery Management System (BMS) functions.
  - Improve battery cooling and thermal safety.

- **Chassis Optimization**
  - Further optimize the AISI 4130 chassis using ANSYS analysis.
  - Reduce unnecessary weight while maintaining structural strength.
  - Improve load distribution and stability.

- **Electrical Safety**
  - Improve High Voltage and Low Voltage system isolation.
  - Enhance emergency shutdown and isolation systems.
  - Improve warning and safety indicators.

- **Thermal Management**
  - Perform detailed thermal analysis of motor, controller and battery.
  - Improve cooling for better continuous performance.

- **Steering and Braking**
  - Further optimize steering geometry.
  - Improve braking response and stopping performance.
  - Validate performance under different operating conditions.

- **Smart Monitoring**
  - Add improved sensors for monitoring important vehicle parameters.
  - Develop better monitoring of battery, motor and electrical systems.

- **Performance Testing**
  - Conduct additional track testing.
  - Study energy consumption, reliability and vehicle performance under different conditions.

- **Manufacturing Improvement**
  - Improve fabrication accuracy and assembly quality.
  - Standardize manufacturing and inspection procedures.

## Future Goal

The future development of the E-Kart is focused on improving **efficiency, safety, reliability, structural performance and energy management** while maintaining a lightweight electric racing vehicle.
